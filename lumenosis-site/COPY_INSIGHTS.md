## 2. Hero Section Principles

**Above-the-fold requirements (all five must be present):**
1. ICP call-out headline (Team Leaders / Broker Owners / Real Estate Teams — pick one per variant)
2. Offer sub-headline (the one outcome + mechanism + timeframe)
3. VSL or founder photo (if no VSL, single high-quality photo — not a stock handshake, not a house graphic)
4. CTA button — "SCHEDULE YOUR DISCOVERY CALL" (never "Get Started," never "Learn More," never "Free Call")
5. Social proof badge — logos of teams/brokerages you've worked with, or press mention ("As seen in WSJ," if applicable)

**Rule: 80% of your conversion effort lives here. If avg session time is under 10 seconds, the ATF section is the only problem to fix.**

**Sub-headline formulas:**

```
Formula A (Pain → Mechanism → Outcome):
"[ICP] are losing [specific loss] to slow follow-up. Our AI front-desk agent replies in under 60 seconds,
qualifies every lead, and books showings automatically — deployed in 14 days."

Formula B (What it does, what you don't need):
"Every inbound inquiry — SMS, email, missed call — handled and qualified in under 60 seconds.
No new staff. No scripts. No dropped leads."

Formula C (Number-led):
"Sub-60s first reply. 12-second average phone pickup. 24/7 coverage on every channel.
Built exclusively for real estate teams."
```

**CTA button text variants (ranked):**
1. SCHEDULE YOUR DISCOVERY CALL NOW
2. BOOK YOUR DEMO CALL
3. SEE HOW IT WORKS

**Never use:** "Get Started Free," "Sign Up," "Contact Us," "Learn More," "Request a Demo" (too generic, lowers intent)

**The word FREE anywhere near your CTA fractures intent and pre-frames the call as a sales pitch, not a diagnostic.**

## 3. VSL / Video Section

**If no VSL yet:** Replace with a single strong photo of the founder or a static image. Remove the poster/thumbnail entirely — do not use a YouTube embed. Use Wistia if you have video.

**VSL script structure (8-12 min, 6 sections):**

```
1. HOOK (10-15 sec): Direct callout
"If you're a real estate team leader and you're losing deals to slow follow-up — keep watching.
This is specifically for you."

2. PROMISE: Frame limiting beliefs as teaching points
"By the end of this video, you'll understand three things your competitors don't about
why speed-to-lead is the only metric that matters right now."

3. WHY ME: 60-90 seconds, founder story + niche proof
"I built Lumenosis because I watched real estate teams spend more time in their CRM
than in front of clients. Here's what I did about it."

4. BELIEF SHIFTING (3 layers):
Layer 1 — Market: "Teams like [Name Brokerage] and [Name Brokerage] are already running
AI front-desk agents. This isn't experimental — it's operational."
Layer 2 — Peers: "We've worked with [ICP type] who were drowning in after-hours inquiries
and are now booking 40% more showings from the same lead volume."
Layer 3 — Them: "If it's happening in your market, and for teams just like yours — the only
question is whether you move first or let your competitor."

5. THE FOUR PATHS (close):
"After watching this, you have four choices. Do nothing — leads keep slipping.
Do it yourself — takes 6 months and still might not work.
Find another agency — plenty of options, none specialized for real estate.
Or work with us — 14-day deployment, 24/7 coverage, results or you don't pay."

6. CTA: "Click the link below and book a 15-minute discovery call. Worst case?
You get a full walkthrough of how this works. No pressure, no pitch."
```

## 4. Awareness Level Copy

**Where real estate team leaders land on the awareness scale when they hit your page:**

Most inbound traffic from LinkedIn or cold outreach = **Problem Aware to Solution Aware**. They feel the pain (lost leads, slow follow-up, agents complaining), they have heard of AI for real estate, but they haven't connected a specific provider to the outcome they want.

A small segment from referrals or repeat touchpoints = **Product Aware** — comparing you to Structurely, Verse, Ylopo.

**Problem Aware copy (the majority):**

```
Lead type: Problem-Solution
Approach: Meet them at the pain. Name it precisely. Present Lumenosis as the obvious answer.

"Have you ever had an agent tell you a lead went cold — only to find out someone else got back
to them first? That's not a lead problem. That's a response time problem."

"The team that replies first wins the client. Right now, yours might not be that team."
```

**Solution Aware copy:**

```
Lead type: Problem-Solution bridge
Approach: They know AI can help. Bridge to your specific outcome.

"You already know you need faster lead response. The question is whether you want a chatbot
that frustrates buyers, or a system that qualifies them and books the showing — automatically."
```

**Product Aware copy (for competitive section or FAQ):**

```
Lead type: Competitive
Approach: Outframe alternatives without naming them.

"You'll see other AI tools that promise more leads. Lumenosis doesn't generate leads.
It makes sure every lead you already have gets a response in under 60 seconds,
gets qualified, and gets booked — so none of them go to a competitor."
```

**Testimonial section title formulas:**
- "Join 12+ Real Estate Teams Who No Longer Chase Cold Leads"
- "How Team Leaders Got Their Evenings Back — and Their Numbers Up"
- "What Happens When Your AI Front-Desk Agent Goes Live"

**What makes a high-converting testimonial (Lumenosis-specific):**
```
Specific result: "We went from a 4-hour average response time to under 2 minutes. Booking rate jumped 31% in the first month."
Emotional transformation: "I stopped dreading Monday mornings. The backlog was gone."
Skeptic-turned: "I thought AI for real estate was hype. Three months in and I can't imagine going back."
```

**Testimonial card structure:**
- Full name + title (Team Leader, Broker Owner, Director of Operations)
- Profile photo (required — no silhouettes)
- Bold keywords in the quote
- Quotation marks

**Grid layout:** 3 columns, minimum 6 testimonials shown. Lead with the strongest result first.

**If testimonials are thin:**
- One strong case study beats nine weak quotes
- Use the format: [Problem] → [Timeframe] → [Outcome]
- "Before Lumenosis, [Team Name] was losing [X] leads a month to slow response. In 14 days, they had a 24/7 AI agent handling inbound. Within 30 days, they booked 18 additional showings from leads that would have gone cold."

**Additional trust signals:**
- Social proof badge above the fold (brokerage logos or brand-name real estate entities)

## 10. CTA Copy

**Page rule:** One CTA per page, repeated at Interest, Desire, and Action sections.

**Primary CTA (all instances):**
```
SCHEDULE YOUR DISCOVERY CALL NOW
```

**Never use:** Free Call, Get Started, Sign Up, Request a Demo, Contact Us

**CTA surrounding copy (the three rules applied):**

```
Rule 1 — Specific next step:
"Scroll down, click the button below, and book a 15-minute discovery call.
We'll show you exactly how this works for your team's lead flow."

Rule 2 — Low-pressure tone:
"Worst case? You get a full walkthrough of how AI front-desk works in a real estate context.
If it's not right for your team, that's absolutely fine — no pressure."

Rule 3 — Genuine urgency (capacity-based, never fake countdown):
"We onboard a limited number of new teams each month to maintain deployment quality.
If we're fully booked, bookmark this page and check back next week. Apologies for the wait."
```

**Button contrast rule:** CTA button color must sharply contrast your brand palette. Contrast is the primary objective — not color psychology.

**Mobile rule:** Full-width sticky CTA button that appears after the first Calendly display.

**Calendar window:** 3-4 days rolling only. Too many slots kills scarcity. Too few kills show-up rate.

**Micro-commitment CTAs (for lower-intent touchpoints):**
- LinkedIn DM: "If this sounds relevant, just reply with a thumbs up and I'll send a quick 2-minute breakdown 👍"
- Email: "Hit reply with 'yes' and I'll send you a short walkthrough of how this works for real estate teams."
- Ad: "Click below to see how this looks in your workflow."
